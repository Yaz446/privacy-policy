Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. Overview

Car Dealer Stock ("the App") is operated by Yasir Janjua. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account information — your name and email address via Google Sign-In
  • Business data — vehicle inventory, financial transactions, expenses, and business settings you enter into the App
  • Contact details — phone numbers and physical addresses entered for your business profile and customer invoices/receipts
  • Photos — images you take of vehicles, stored locally and in your Google Drive
  • Identity verification photos — front-camera photos captured when a customer signs a purchase receipt, saved to your Google Drive only
  • Device information — basic usage data collected by Firebase

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide car stock management and financial tracking features
  • To sync your data across devices via Firebase
  • To back up your data to your personal Google Drive account
  • To generate invoices, receipts, and reports

4. Data Storage

Your business data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) and your personal Google Drive. We do not have access to your Google Drive files. Financial data is never shared with third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In & Firebase (Google LLC)
  • Google Drive API (Google LLC)
  • Google Places API (Google LLC)
  • DVLA Vehicle Enquiry API (UK Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency)

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties outside of the services listed above.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you maintain your account. You can delete your data at any time by clearing the app and removing your Google Drive backup files.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact us at the email below to make a request.

9. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for business use only and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Contact
